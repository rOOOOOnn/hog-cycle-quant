# Research Plan

## Research Philosophy

The project follows a fundamental quant approach:

Fundamental Model + Market Pricing Model = Trading Signal

The goal is not forecasting price direction alone, but identifying mispricing.

---

## V1 Research Questions

1. Do livestock biological cycles predict future hog prices?
2. What are the optimal lags for:
   - sow inventory
   - piglet prices
   - farming margins
3. Can fundamental indicators predict future price regimes?
